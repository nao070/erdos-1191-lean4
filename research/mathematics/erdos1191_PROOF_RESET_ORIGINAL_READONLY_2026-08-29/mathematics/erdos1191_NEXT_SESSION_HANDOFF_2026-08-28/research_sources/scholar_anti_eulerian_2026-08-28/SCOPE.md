# Scholar scope — critical anti-Eulerian budget

**Question.** Which established theorem or technique can prove a
sub-logarithmic signed cyclic-arc covariance budget across critical-density
dyadic prefixes of an infinite additive Sidon sequence, or rigorously show
that this proposed route cannot work?

**Archetype:** literature review targeted at a proof mechanism, not a general
historical survey.

## Decomposition

- **Problem:** infinite additive Sidon sequences under the hypothetical
  envelope `a_m <= C m^2 log m`.
- **Intervention/method:** multiscale signed covariance, inverse cycle
  Laplacians, martingale square functions, entropy, discrepancy, large sieve,
  or finite-Sidon Fourier uniformity.
- **Comparison:** the existing one-scale block-energy/Cauchy architecture and
  the exact endpoint-variance functional already certified in this workspace.
- **Outcome:** a uniform `o(log J)` upper budget, a weaker structural lemma
  that implies it, or a counterexample showing the functional must be changed.

## Keyword clusters

1. `infinite additive Sidon sequence` AND (`martingale` OR `entropy` OR
   `multiscale` OR `square function`) AND (`density` OR `counting function`).
2. (`cyclic interval coverage` OR `arc covariance` OR `circular discrepancy`)
   AND (`inverse Laplacian` OR `effective resistance` OR `negative Sobolev` OR
   `H^-1`).
3. (`Sidon set` OR `Golomb ruler`) AND (`large sieve` OR `Fourier uniformity`
   OR `endpoint quadruple` OR `additive energy`) AND (`local` OR `residue`).
4. Exact authors/results: Erdős, Ruzsa, Cilleruelo, O'Bryant,
   Ortega--Prendiville, Ding, Táfula, and current citing/cited work.

**Negative cluster:** exclude harmonic-analysis/compact-group Sidon sets,
multiplicative Sidon sets, dynamical Sidon constructions, and generalized
`B_h[g]` results without an explicit `h=2,g=1` reduction.

Every semantic/index result is discovery only.  A theorem enters the project
only after checking the primary paper and its exact hypotheses.
