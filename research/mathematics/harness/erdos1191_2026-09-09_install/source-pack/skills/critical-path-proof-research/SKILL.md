# Critical-Path Proof Research

## Core rule
Work the **current exact theorem**, preserve failed mathematics, and optimize for new mathematical information rather than activity.

## Start
Read `research/NEXT_THEOREM_CONTRACT.md`, then only its direct dependencies and the most relevant prior attempts. Confirm the theorem ID and quantifier order before deriving anything. Do not restart pre-flight unless a concrete contradiction is found.

## One attack at a time
State one falsifiable mathematical move: a quantified lemma, explicit inequality, representation, potential, or counterexample mechanism. Record an attempt fingerprint based on the **mechanism**, not notation.

Before long work, probe the smallest admissible ranks, degenerate cases, signs, constant dependencies, horizon/order-of-limit issues, and known exact fixtures when relevant.

Then actually derive or break the candidate. A plan, literature list, declaration count, or rephrasing of the original existence of `K` is not progress by itself.

## Attempt record
Preserve the load-bearing derivation/experiment and append:

```yaml
attempt_id:
target:
mechanism:
exact_claim:
assumptions:
evidence:
first_unproved_or_false_step:
failure_class:
new_information:
sibling_difference:
next_nonduplicate_action:
```

A summary never replaces raw evidence. A retry is allowed only if `sibling_difference` names a changed mathematical ingredient.

## Route discipline
A false **stronger sufficient estimate** kills that estimate, not the frozen theorem. A genuine refutation of the frozen theorem must satisfy its exact negation and then be connected through the finite-tree/cap logic recorded in the contract.

Keep U4-E/U4-R/U4-G dormant while U4-F is producing new information. Re-open an alternative only after documenting why the active mechanism is exhausted or why another route now has a shorter critical path.

## Escalation
Use a fresh falsifier before promoting a route-changing claim. Use Lean when a statement has stabilized or Lean can resolve a fragile interface. Continue to the next mathematical action unless the master completion gate is actually met or execution is externally stopped.
