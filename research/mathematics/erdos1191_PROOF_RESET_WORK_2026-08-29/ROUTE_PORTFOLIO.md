## 4. Route C — nonlinear multiscale architecture

### Proved G2 no-go for independent linear multi-`N` averaging

Suppose each block size separately gives

\[
 E_N\le u_N,
 \qquad
 E_N\ge c_N\tau^2,
 \qquad c_N>0.
\]

For arbitrary nonnegative weights `lambda_N`, independent addition yields

\[
 \tau^2\le
 {\sum_N\lambda_Nu_N\over\sum_N\lambda_Nc_N}
 =\sum_N
 {\lambda_Nc_N\over\sum_M\lambda_Mc_M}{u_N\over c_N}.
\tag{C.1}
\]

The right side is a `c`-weighted convex combination of the one-scale ratios
`u_N/c_N`, hence it is never below their minimum.  Independent linear
multi-`N` averaging cannot beat the best single `N`.  This is an exact **G2
subroute closure**, consistent with the multi-block suggestion in O'Bryant
Section 3.1 but showing what extra mechanism that suggestion must contain.

### Primary-source escape model: Hou--Zhao joint feasibility

[Hou--Zhao, arXiv:2607.01169v2, Sections 2--4](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01169v2)
give a genuine escape from (C.1), but only for the finite Sidon problem.  They
place several smoothed indicators in a weighted Hilbert direct sum.  Crucially,
an individual kernel--boundary pair need not satisfy the boundary covering
condition; only the weighted combination is feasible.  Cauchy--Schwarz is
then applied to the joint object and gives the exact diagonal/direct-sum
master inequality

\[
 k^2\le (N+bH-1)\left(1+{a(k-1)\over H}\right).
\tag{C.2}
\]

Thus (C.2) is not a convex average of independently valid scalar upper bounds,
and it does not contradict the no-go (C.1).  The current v2 proves the finite
bound `F(N)<=N^(1/2)+0.9435N^(1/4)+O(1)` and, in
[Section 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01169v2), identifies controlled
cross-kernel terms as a possible extension while adding an important
qualification: a positive-semidefinite coefficient matrix alone is not
enough; the combined correlation kernel must be nonnegative at every nonzero
shift.  This is finite `N^(1/4)` second-order progress, not direct progress on
Question 1 or Question 2.

The companion [exact cross-kernel positivity gate](route_probes/ROUTE_C_CROSS_KERNEL_GATE.md)
makes that caveat algebraic.  For
`E_H=sum_(r,s)h_rs<1_A*K_r,1_A*K_s>`, it proves

\[
 E_H=|A|C_H(0)+2\sum_{d\in\Delta(A)}C_H(d).
\tag{C.3}
\]

Filling unrepresented Sidon differences in the upper bound is legal only
when `C_H(d)>=0` at every nonzero shift, or after paying an explicit
positive-part cost.  Positive semidefiniteness of `H` alone fails: with
`H=[[1,-1/2],[-1/2,1]]`, `K_1=delta_0`, `K_2=delta_1`, and `A={0,2}`, the
actual energy is `4` while the illicit PSD-only upper bound is `3`.

Accordingly, a viable #1191 multi-`N` route must imitate the joint mechanism:
use a covering constraint that is feasible only after scales cooperate, a
common-shift cross-correlation/orthogonality term, an entropy chain-rule
deficit, or an incompatibility theorem for near-equality profiles.  Merely
adding separately valid one-scale inequalities remains closed by (C.1).
