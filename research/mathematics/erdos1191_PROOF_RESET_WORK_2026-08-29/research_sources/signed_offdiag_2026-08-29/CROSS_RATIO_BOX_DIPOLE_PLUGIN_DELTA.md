## Closest primary precedents

### Frerick--Müller--Thomaser

Leonhard Frerick, Jürgen Müller, and Tobias Thomaser,
*A Fourier Integral Formula for Logarithmic Energy*, Potential Analysis
(2024), DOI
[`10.1007/s11118-024-10125-9`](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11118-024-10125-9),
[arXiv:2209.05439](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05439).

Theorem 1.1 supplies a Fourier representation of mutual logarithmic energy
with the necessary total-mass renormalization.  In the zero-total-mass
one-dimensional specialization the kernel is proportional, subject to the
paper's Fourier convention, to `d xi/|xi|`.  This is the closest spectral
precedent for the dipole calculation.  The paper's finite-self-energy
corollary must not be applied directly to an atomic dipole, whose self-energy
is infinite; the present project uses a finite mutual interaction between
two separated dipoles and also proves the box integral elementarily.

### Bacry--Muzy

Emmanuel Bacry and Jean-François Muzy,
*Log-Infinitely Divisible Multifractal Processes*, Communications in
Mathematical Physics 236 (2003), DOI
[`10.1007/s00220-003-0827-3`](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-003-0827-3),
[arXiv:cond-mat/0207094](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0207094).

Their independently scattered measure lives on a time-scale half-plane with
control measure `dt dl/l^2`; cone cross-sections are intervals.  For the
exact-scaling choice `f(l)=l` below the integral scale and `f(l)=T` above it,
the cone-overlap covariance is computed explicitly and contains the
logarithm.  The finite-scale triangular part gives

\[
 \log(T/t)-1+t/T,
\]

while the large-scale plateau contributes the missing `1-t/T`.  This is a
direct precedent for the project's finite-horizon potential

\[
 F_T(t)=\log(T/t)+t/T-1
\]

and confirms that an exact pure logarithm needs an explicit terminal or
large-scale contribution.  It does not supply the Sidon ownership theorem.

### Gneiting

Tilmann Gneiting, *Radial Positive Definite Functions Generated by Euclid's
Hat*, Journal of Multivariate Analysis 69 (1999), DOI
[`10.1006/jmva.1998.1800`](https://doi.org/10.1006/jmva.1998.1800).

Euclid's hat is a normalized self-convolution of a ball indicator.  In one
dimension it is the triangular interval autocorrelation
`(1-|x|/T)_+`, up to scale normalization.  This supports the standard
positive-definite status of the box-overlap kernel `R_T`; it does not state
the four-point cross-ratio combination.

### Junnila--Saksman--Webb

Janne Junnila, Eero Saksman, and Christian Webb,
*Decompositions of Log-Correlated Fields with Applications*, Annals of
Applied Probability 30 (2020), DOI
[`10.1214/19-AAP1492`](https://doi.org/10.1214/19-AAP1492),
[arXiv:1808.06838](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.06838).

Their scale-integral construction starts from a positive-definite seed and
builds a log-correlated covariance with independent scale increments.  With
the one-dimensional triangular seed this is the same interval-overlap
architecture, generally with a smooth boundary correction rather than the
project's exact four-point finite-horizon identity.

### Duplantier--Rhodes--Sheffield--Vargas

Bertrand Duplantier, Rémi Rhodes, Scott Sheffield, and Vincent Vargas,
*Log-Correlated Gaussian Fields: An Overview*,
[arXiv:1407.5605](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.5605), DOI
[`10.1007/978-3-319-49638-2_9`](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-49638-2_9).

The overview defines the logarithmic field on mean-zero test functions and
records continuous scale decompositions from compactly supported
positive-definite seeds.  It supplies the established interpretation of
dipoles as zero-mass probes, not the project-specific Sidon consequence.

## Spectral consistency check

For the continuous normalized interval box,

\[
 \widehat K_T(\xi)
 =e^{-i\xi T/2}{2\sin(\xi T/2)\over\xi T},
\]

and the elementary scale integral is

\[
 \int_0^\infty|\widehat K_T(\xi)|^2\,dT
 ={\pi\over|\xi|}
\]

under the corresponding Fourier convention.  Parseval therefore makes the
project's box-dipole identity consistent with the Fourier logarithmic-energy
formula.  The project does not depend on this citation: the tent formula and
its integral are proved directly and exactly in
`route_probes/ROUTE_C_CROSS_RATIO_BOX_DIPOLE_BRIDGE.md`.

## Qualified result

The checked sources contain the ingredients separately: Fourier logarithmic
energy, triangular interval covariance, cone-overlap logarithms, and
continuous scale decompositions.  No checked primary source stated the exact
four-point formula in the displayed box-dipole form, its exact log-phase
dyadic periodization, or a finite-horizon Sidon/Golomb signed-capacity theorem
using it.

This is a dated targeted retrieval null, not a theorem of absence and not a
novelty claim.  The log-phase formula is an elementary change of variables;
the box-dipole formula should presently be described as a clean synthesis of
known ingredients.  Any potentially publishable contribution would have to
come from a correct no-double-counting insertion into the #1191 ledger, which
remains open.
