## Database-specific

### zbMATH / MathSciNet
- exact title + author + DOI;

### EuDML / Numdam / Math-Net.Ru
- add `additive`, `B_2`, `integer sequence`, `difference`, `counting function`;
- reject compact-group/harmonic-analysis/spectral “Sidon” results unless an explicit transfer theorem exists.
